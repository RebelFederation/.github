# Code of Conduct

## Our standard

We are committed to providing a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free community for everyone.

Expected behavior includes:

- Being respectful and constructive.
- Assuming good intent while addressing impact.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Respecting different viewpoints and experiences.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, and publishing private information without permission.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the project maintainers through the contact method listed in the repository's security policy. Reports will be reviewed and handled as confidentially as practical.
